**Goal:** the app builds a real building: a walking skeleton first (one small house end to end —
spec → foundation + one floor with rebar → one drawing sheet → BOQ), then scale to multi-storey
with MEP and federation; the NativeCAD workspace and the full-CAD tab.

**Timebox:** weeks 10–17 (plan §10, started 2026-10-20).

small house: spec → foundation + floor with rebar → sheet → BOQ) and **W2.11** is the
equivalence gate (incremental ≡ full rebuild) in CI.
**Plan of record:** `docs/plan/ArchResidenceBD-Plan-v1.5.md` §5–§6, §8, §9, §10, Appendix C.
**Handover from P1:** `docs/work/P1-EXIT-REVIEW.md` (what P1 delivered, the declared gaps, the

The BOQ is a frozen interface (`spec/schemas/boq.schema.json`, generated into the Python/TS interfaces).
  **The bug the arithmetic caught:** the first build priced the excavation as a **volume** with the excavator's **hourly** rate — 293.9 m³ × 2,800 BDT/h = 822,899 BDT, 94 % of the BOQ for a meaningless number. Two rules now prevent it: the line is measured in **hours**, and **a rate in another unit never prices a line** (it stays visible and unpriced, with the mismatch in its note). A line the card cannot price (the formwork) also stays visible, and the **candidate** card says on the document that these prices are not a quotation (ADR-0028).

- [x] **W2.7 Price service** — official lists, override, regional multipliers; machinery rates. ∥ — **implemented 2026-10-21:** `core/src/archres_core/pricing.py` is the path from a **rate card** to a **price**: the `CardRegistry` (the cards the app knows, the files that are not cards skipped **and reported**), the district → region bridge, the resolution for the project's region, the pricing block pushed into the Building Spec, the user's own rate registered in the Deviation Register, and the consent-gated fetch of an official list through the Source Registry (W0.10) with a recipe-driven parser (ADR-0030).
  **The three laws the service keeps:** a **regional multiplier is published or assumed, never silent** (the card's own region is 1.0; a published factor is applied; a region the card says nothing about is priced at 1.00 and marked `source: default` — a price that pretends to be regional is worse than a missing one); a **fetched list enters as a candidate** (consent first, the artifact hashed, the card `candidate` until a human marks it active, and a row without a reviewed Bangla **and** English label is not a rate line); and a **price override is registered, not applied silently** (the pricing entry says `source: user` and names the card rate it replaced, and the difference goes into the Deviation Register with the reason, the acknowledgement and the nearest compliant alternative).
  **Measured** (`data/prices/`, the report in `data/prices/report.md`): the registry holds **1 card** (`dhaka-example-2026-10`, dhaka, candidate, 8 lines) and reports the **4** non-card files it skipped; the resolution for Dhaka leaves every rate unchanged, for Chattogram applies the card's published **1.06** (cement 550 → 583.00, rod 89,000 → 94,340.00) and for Rajshahi (no published factor) prices at 1.00 with the assumption on the line; the priced spec carries the `/pricing` block **and** the spec's own provenance entry for it; and the **cross-check: the BOQ priced from the resolved card totals 119,599.70 BDT — the same as the BOQ priced straight from the card.** The machinery law is honoured too: the card's "minimum 2 h per mobilization" is billed (and the line says why), and it is **not** applied to a per-day rate (a unit error).

Notes:

- **Package-closure checklist (unchanged from P1).** A package is not done when the code lands; it is
  done when **all** of these hold, in order:
  1. the full local gate sweep is green — `pytest`, `tools/ci/py_syntax_check.py`,
     `tools/ci/brand_guard.py`, `generate_spec.py --check`, `generate_interfaces.py --check`,
     and every `--check` freshness CLI relevant to the package;
  2. where models or CAD are touched, a **live probe is recorded in the spec doc** with its
     real output — a stubbed transport is a test, not a verification;
  3. the board and spec numbers are true (or the exact count is removed from prose and left
     to CI output — hand-maintained totals drift);
  4. the package is committed, pushed, and CI is green on the commit;
  5. only then is the box on this board ticked — **last**, never first.
- **Quality law (plan §9) applies to CAD like everywhere else:** a measured geometry claim must be
  reproducible (the W1.8 harness demands identical verdict signatures and artifact trees on a
  re-run), a missing capability is an explicit visible state, and anything not executed and
  measured is a candidate.
- **Declared gaps from the plan (2026-10-21 audit).** The plan's §9 names four CI guards and
  Appendix A carries a feature bench; three of them have no package yet, and the audit records
  that rather than letting them sit implied: **spec fuzzing** (§9) and the **Bangla rendering
  regression** (glyph shaping, numerals, mixed BN+EN on generated PDFs — the first Bangla PDF
  arrives with P3's reports) have no WBS entry; the **incremental ≡ full-rebuild equivalence**
  gate is W2.11; Appendix A2's bench (Bangla voice notes, "Mistri sheets", the construction
  progress tracker, site photos, the in-app engineer round-trip) is approved but unassigned.
  The plan's §3 requirements that are *not* P2's are the other-city packs (P4/W4.5) and the
